Getting started:
1. Launch Simple Account Balancer and enter your starting balance as of a date.
2. Log each debit or credit the moment it happens. The header balance is always your real balance.
3. Use the Compare tab with your bank's optional posted balance. Mark entries Found as you match them. Found marks are temporary and never change your register. If there is an unexplained difference, use the finder to help track it down.

Backups and restore:
- The app backs up every account automatically when it opens and closes. No backup is taken while there are no accounts yet.
- Save backup... in account settings writes a copy of your data to any place you pick, such as a USB drive.
- Restore from backup in account settings lists the automatic backups. Restore from a file... on that list takes a backup .db file from anywhere. The app takes a safety backup of your current data first.
- A backup made by a newer version of the app is refused. Update the app first, then restore it.
- Backups hold your data only. App settings, such as the backup folder and window position, stay with each computer.

Moving to a new computer or reinstalling:
1. On the old computer, use Save backup... in account settings.
2. Install the app on the new computer and open it.
3. On the first-launch screen, choose Restore from a backup... and pick the saved file. A backup with no accounts in it is refused.
